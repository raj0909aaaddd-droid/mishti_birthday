Dear Misht, 🌹

I tried writing this a few different ways, and every version sounded either too serious or too much like a card. So I’ll just say it plainly.

Thank you for being someone it’s easy to be around. That sounds small, but it’s rare. You make rooms feel lighter without making a show of it, and you listen in a way that makes people feel they weren’t talking into the void.

I hope this year is gentle where you need gentleness, and brave where you need courage. I hope you get the quiet mornings you’ve been wanting, and the loud, ridiculous evenings too. I hope the things you’re working toward finally start answering back.

And on the days you doubt yourself, because everyone does, I hope you remember you’re already someone people are glad to know. Not someday. Now.

You don’t need to become anything new for this birthday. Just keep being the person someone wanted to build all of this for.

Happy birthday, Misht. I’m really glad you exist. 💛

— With love
